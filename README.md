**Project Focus**

Key Design Principles:

🎨 Attractive and consistent visual design

🧭 Simple and intuitive navigation

🍕 High-quality food imagery

🛒 Easy food selection and ordering

💳 Clear checkout and payment experience

🗺️ User-friendly delivery tracking

📱 Consistent typography, spacing, and visual hierarchy

👤 User-centered experience

**Project Goal**

To design an attractive, intuitive, and user-friendly food delivery app that allows users to explore food categories, discover delicious meals, place orders conveniently, track deliveries, and rate their overall experience.
